| **Symptoms** | **Probable Cause** | **Action** |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ---------- |
| Application rejected; message about GitHub account security | Your GitHub account does not have **_two-factor authentication_** enabled | Go to your GitHub account settings, navigate to **Security**, and enable two-factor authentication. You may need to log out and log back in before reapplying. |
| Application rejected; message about billing information | Your **_GitHub billing information_** is incomplete or does not match your academic affiliation documents | Complete your GitHub billing information with your full legal name exactly as it appears in your academic affiliation document. Ensure the information matches your school records. Log out and log back in before reapplying. |
| Application rejected; message about contributions | You don't have enough contributions to GitHub or GitHub Classroom assignments | Try applying again after making more contributions to any **_GitHub Classroom_** assignments created by your teacher or demonstrating activity on GitHub. |
| Application rejected; message about profile verification | Your **_GitHub user profile_** is incomplete or inaccurate | Complete your GitHub user profile with your full name exactly as it appears in your academic affiliation document. Do not use a variation of your name or a nickname. Log out and log back in before reapplying. |
| Emails from Learning Hub still appearing in inbox | Your filter may be **_disabled_**, or the domain filter doesn't match the sender address | Go to **Settings > Filters**, verify that your filter is **_active_** (toggle is **_off_**). Ensure your filter condition uses `mail.learn.bcit.ca` as the sender address, not the full email address. |
| Filter not catching Learning Hub emails | You may have entered the wrong email address or domain in the filter condition | Go to **Settings > Filters**, edit your filter, and verify that you used `mail.learn.bcit.ca` in the _text field_, not the full sender address starting with "no-reply@". |
| Can't find archived emails | Archived emails are moved out of your inbox but remain in your mailbox _Archive_ folder | Check your mailbox folder list on the left side of your email. Look for the _Archive_ folder, or use the **Search** function to locate your archived messages. |
| Filter applying to all emails instead of just Learning Hub | You may have selected the **all messages** condition instead of creating a specific rule | Edit your filter in **Settings > Filters**. Make sure you selected **matching any of the following rules** or **matching all of the following rules**, not **all messages**. |
| Filter isn't working anymore | The **_Filter disabled_** toggle may have been accidentally turned **_on_** | Go to **Settings > Filters**, select your filter, and verify that the **_Filter disabled_** toggle in the bottom-right is **_off_** to activate the filter. |
| Multiple filters conflicting with each other | You may have multiple filters with overlapping conditions that interfere with each other | Go to **Settings > Filters** and review all your active filters. Check that your filter conditions are specific and don't overlap. You may need to edit or delete conflicting filters. |
