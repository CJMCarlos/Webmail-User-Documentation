## **Overview**

The GitHub Student Developer Pack is a collection of free tools and services provided by GitHub for students. It includes free GitHub Pro, access to educational resources, cloud credits, and more. As a BCIT student, you're eligible to claim these valuable benefits.

## **Getting GitHub Student Developer Pack**

Ready to unlock free developer tools? The GitHub Student Developer Pack includes everything from GitHub Pro to cloud credits and design tools!

Here are the steps to get started!

### **Preparing Your GitHub Account** {.subheader}

1. Go to the [GitHub Education website](https://education.github.com/pack).

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub1.png"GitHub Education homepage")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub1.gif){width=600px}

2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** if you already have an account, or **Sign up** to create a new one.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub2.png"GitHub sign in button")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub2.gif){width=600px}

    !!! info
        If you're creating a new account, make sure to use your **BCIT email address** to verify your student status later.

3. Complete the GitHub account setup if you're new to the platform.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub3.png"GitHub signup form")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub3.gif){width=600px}

### **Verifying Your Student Status** {.subheader}

4. Once logged in, click the **Get benefits** button.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub4.png"Get benefits button")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub4.gif){width=600px}

5. Click **Verify your academic status**.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub5.png"Academic status verification")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub5.gif){width=600px}

6. Select your country and school.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub6.png"Country and school selection")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub6.gif){width=600px}

    !!! info
        Search for **BCIT** (British Columbia Institute of Technology) in the school list.

### **Submitting Proof of Status** {.subheader}

7. Select your method of verification. Choose from:
    - Your school-provided email address
    - School ID or enrollment documentation

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub7.png"Verification method options")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub7.gif){width=600px}

8. If using email verification, enter your **BCIT email address**.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub8.png"BCIT email entry")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub8.gif){width=600px}

    !!! warning
        You must use your BCIT email address (ending in @student.bcit.ca or @bcit.ca) to verify your student status.

9. A verification email will be sent to your BCIT email. Click the confirmation link.

    === "Image"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub9.png"Confirmation email")

    === "Gif"
        ![alt text](assets/GitHub9.gif){width=600px}

    !!! info
        Check your email's spam folder if you don't see the verification email within a few minutes.

### **Unlocking Your Benefits** {.subheader}

10. Return to GitHub Education after confirming your email.

    === "Image"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub10.png"Back to GitHub Education")

    === "Gif"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub10.gif){width=600px}

11. Your status should now show as **Verified Student**. Explore the available benefits listed on the page.

    === "Image"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub11.png"Verified student status")

    === "Gif"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub11.gif){width=600px}

12. Claim the individual benefits you want by visiting their respective pages and activating them.

    === "Image"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub12.png"Benefits list")

    === "Gif"
    ![alt text](assets/GitHub12.gif){width=600px}

    !!! success
        You've successfully joined the GitHub Student Developer Pack! Start exploring your free tools and resources.

## **Conclusion**

By the end of this guide, you will have successfully:

✅ Created or signed into your GitHub account  
✅ Verified your student status with your BCIT email  
✅ Unlocked access to the GitHub Student Developer Pack  
✅ Gained access to free premium tools and services

The GitHub Student Developer Pack provides incredible value during your time at BCIT. Take advantage of tools like GitHub Pro, cloud credits, design software, and more. These resources will enhance your learning and development skills throughout your studies.

For more information about individual benefits and how to use them, visit the [GitHub Education website](https://education.github.com/pack).
