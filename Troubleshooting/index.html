    Troubleshooting

    Symptoms Probable Cause Action
    Application rejected; message about GitHub account security Your GitHub account does not have two-factor authentication enabled Go to your GitHub account settings, navigate to Security, and enable two-factor authentication. You may need to log out and log back in before reapplying.
    Application rejected; message about billing information Your GitHub billing information is incomplete or does not match your academic affiliation documents Complete your GitHub billing information with your full legal name exactly as it appears in your academic affiliation document. Ensure the information matches your school records. Log out and log back in before reapplying.
    Application rejected; message about contributions You don't have enough contributions to GitHub or GitHub Classroom assignments Try applying again after making more contributions to any GitHub Classroom assignments created by your teacher or demonstrating activity on GitHub.
    Application rejected; message about profile verification Your GitHub user profile is incomplete or inaccurate Complete your GitHub user profile with your full name exactly as it appears in your academic affiliation document. Do not use a variation of your name or a nickname. Log out and log back in before reapplying.
    Emails from Learning Hub still appearing in inbox Your filter may be disabled, or the domain filter doesn't match the sender address Go to Settings > Filters, verify that your filter is active (toggle is off). Ensure your filter condition uses mail.learn.bcit.ca as the sender address, not the full email address.
    Filter not catching Learning Hub emails You may have entered the wrong email address or domain in the filter condition Go to Settings > Filters, edit your filter, and verify that you used mail.learn.bcit.ca in the text field, not the full sender address starting with "no-reply@".
    Can't find archived emails Archived emails are moved out of your inbox but remain in your mailbox Archive folder Check your mailbox folder list on the left side of your email. Look for the Archive folder, or use the Search function to locate your archived messages.
    Filter applying to all emails instead of just Learning Hub You may have selected the all messages condition instead of creating a specific rule Edit your filter in Settings > Filters. Make sure you selected matching any of the following rules or matching all of the following rules, not all messages.
    Filter isn't working anymore The Filter disabled toggle may have been accidentally turned on Go to Settings > Filters, select your filter, and verify that the Filter disabled toggle in the bottom-right is off to activate the filter.
    Multiple filters conflicting with each other You may have multiple filters with overlapping conditions that interfere with each other Go to Settings > Filters and review all your active filters. Check that your filter conditions are specific and don't overlap. You may need to edit or delete conflicting filters.
